Delete your account & data

This page explains how to delete your Skippy account (developer: Dor Segal) and all associated data.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

Open Skippy → Profile screen → tap "Delete account" (מחק חשבון) and confirm. Your account and all data are erased immediately and permanently.

Option 2 — If you can't access the app

Email shegs.studio@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Account deletion". We complete the deletion within 7 days.

What is deleted

Account details (email address, name, user ID); all study data (your answers, exam attempts, and progress); usage and crash reports linked to the account. For Sign in with Apple users, the Apple sign-in token is also revoked on Apple's servers.

Data retention

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. We retain no data after deletion except where retention is required by law. There is no additional retention period.
